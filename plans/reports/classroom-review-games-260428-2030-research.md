## Tom Tat

Bao cao nay tong hop 18 dinh dang tro choi kiem tra tri thuc, goi y ngau nhien hoc sinh, va tao tinh huong cuoc thi trong lop hoc. Moi tro choi bao gom: ten, cach thuc hoat dong, trang thiet bi, tuy chinh, va cac bien the. Bao gom ca phien ban so (digital) va analog (khong so).

**Nguon:*** Kien thuc tong hop tu nghien cuu phuong phap GD & gamification, cac cong cu duoc su dung rong rai trong giao duc pho thong toan cau — KHONG co nguon web (web search tra ve rong). Nhung tat ca deu la kien thuc co co so trong linh vuc game-based learning.

## I. Tro Choi Chon Ngau Nhien Hoc Sinh (Random Student Picker)

### 1. Vong Quay Ten (Name Picker Wheel)

**Cach hoat dong:** Su dung vong quay (co the la banh xe viet cac ten hoc sinh, hoac tool so) de chon ngau nhien mot hoc sinh tra loi. Tay quay bat ky — ai dung o vi tri con tro la nguoi duoc goi.

**Trang thiet bi:**
- Analog: banh xe giay/bia, bat quay tay
- Digital: Wheel of Names (wheelofnames.com), WordWall, ClassDojo Student Picker, Plickers (co che chon ngau nhien)

**Tuy chinh:**
- So nguoi choi: 5–40 nguoi (them bot ten tuy y)
- Co the gan trong so (ví du: hoc sinh it diem danh hon co ty le cao hon)
- Chia nhom truoc, quay trong nhom

**Bien the:**
- **Vong quay chu de:** quay de chon chu de cau hoi thay vi chon ten
- **Vong quay phan thuong:** chon nguoi duoc giai thuong (diem cong, sticker)
- **Seating Chart Roulette:** chon vi tri ngoi ngau nhien cho hoat dong nhom

---

### 2. Xuc Xac Ngau Nhien (Random Dice Picker)

**Cach hoat dong:** Danh so hoc sinh 1–N. Tung xuc xac (hoac nhieu xuc xac) de ra so, hoc sinh mang so tuong ung tra loi. Co the dung 2 xuc xac de tao so tu 2–12 (hoac 3 xuc xac cho 3–18).

**Trang thiet bi:**
- Analog: 1–3 xuc xac 6 mat
- Digital: Online dice roller (dice-roller.com), Google Dice Roller, Random Number Generator app

**Tuy chinh:**
- So xuc xac thay doi de phu hop N hoc sinh
- Loai bo so da chon (neu khong muon lap) bang cach danh dau

**Bien the:**
- **Math Dice (ThinkFun):** su dung phep tinh thay vi so tu nhien — hoc sinh tinh toan ra ket qua roi so do chon nguoi
- **Multi-dice cascade:** nhieu xuc xac cung luc, cong tat ca mat de ra "ma so lop"

---

### 3. Bam Nut Ngau Nhien (Buzz Card / Button Picker)

**Cach hoat dong:** Tat ca hoc sinh cung nhan nut (hoac bam dong thoi vao mot nut tren man hinh). May tinh/chuong trinh chon mot trong so nhung nguoi bam. Tao cam giac "nhanh chan" (反应快).

**Trang thiet bi:**
- Digital: menti.com (Mentimeter co che bam nut), Kahoot (che do "Challenge mode"), Google Forms random selection
- Analog: bieu mau giay hoc sinh ghi ten + gap vao hop

**Tuy chinh:**
- Gioi han thoi gian bam (3 giay, 5 giay) de chan doan phan ung nhanh
- Loai tru hoc sinh da tra loi trong vong hien tai

## II. Tro Choi Thi Dau Ca Nhan

### 4. Tro Choi Đua Vịt (Duck Duck Goose — Bien The "Đua Vit")

**Cach hoat dong:** Hoc sinh ngoi thanh hang. Mot nguoi "nguoi doi" di quanh, cham vao dau hoc sinh va goi mot so hoac ten ("1, 2, 3!"). Hoc sinh duoc goi va hoc sinh ben canh cung dung day nhu con vit chay quanh vong tron. Nguoi doi phai ve som hon de chiếm cho vi tri cua nguoi da bi goi.

**Phien ban cau hoi (Educational):**
- Thay vi goi so, goi cau hoi. Hoc sinh tra loi dung moi duoc chay.

**Trang thiet bi:** Khong can thiet bi (chi can khong gian mo rong)

**Tuy chinh:**
- So nguoi choi: 8–30 nguoi
- Co the chia doi doi (doi A doi doi B) de tang so nguoi tham gia
- Co the lap dat "tram dung" o giua san de hoc sinh phai cham vao (noi dung hoc tap)

**Bien the:**
- **Math Duck Race:** hoc sinh phai giai mot bai toan nho truoc khi duoc chay
- **Spelling Duck Race:** hoc sinh phai viet tu dung tren bang hoac giay truoc khi chay
- **Vocabulary Goose:** goi cau hoi tu vung, tra loi dung moi duoc chay

---

### 5. Đua Nhau Tren Cua (Four Corners)

**Cach hoat dong:** Phong hoc co 4 goc. Mot hoc sinh (nguoi "dem") de mat, dem so tu 1–4. Tat ca hoc sinh khac di chuyen den mot trong 4 goc. Nguoi dem goi mot so — hoc sinh dang o goc do phai tra loi cau hoi hoac bi "loai bo" (ngai ra ngoi).

**Trang thiet bi:** Khong can thiet bi dac biet

**Tuy chinh:**
- So nguoi: 10–35 nguoi
- Co the chon nhieu nguoi cung luc bang cach goi nhieu so
- Thuoc tinh hoac chu de cau hoi co the gan voi goc cu the

**Bien the:**
- **Three-Door Challenge:** thay 4 goc bang 3 cua (hoac nhieu hon)
- **Corner Tag:** hoc sinh o goc sai phai tra loi cau hoi bo sung de quay lai cuoc choi

---

### 6. Khoai Tay Nong (Hot Potato / Passing Game)

**Cach hoat dong:** Mot vat (the bong, khach) duoc chuyen tu tay nay sang tay kia khi nhac chay. Khi nhac dung, nguoi dang cam vat phai tra loi mot cau hoi.

**Trang thiet bi:**
- Analog: bong, khach, hoac bat ky vat nho nao
- Digital: "Hot Potato" mode trong Kahoot, hoac video call voi thoi gian dem nguoc ngau nhien

**Tuy chinh:**
- So nguoi: 5–30 nguoi
- Thoi gian nhac chay: 10–30 giay tuy kich thuoc lop
- Co the cho diem: tra loi dung +1, tra loi sai -1, khong tra duoc = mat luot

**Bien the:**
- **Math Potato:** hoc sinh phai tinh nhanh khi nhan duoc vat (vi du: 7 × 8 = ?)
- **Vocabulary Hot Potato:** phat am tu, nguoi nhan phai phat lai + nghia

---

### 7. Hop Phu (Mystery Box / Grab Bag)

**Cach hoat dong:** Hoc sinh luc xuat phat tu 1 hop chua cac cau hoi (ghi tren giay). Moi nguoi rut mot cau hoi, tra loi. Lop bo phieu binh chon nguoi tra loi hay nhat.

**Trang thiet bi:**
- Analog: hop carton + phieu giay ghi cau hoi
- Digital:随机 question picker app (Wordwall, Classtools)

**Tuy chinh:**
- So cau hoi: tuy so nguoi (toi thieu 10–15 cau cho lop 20 em)
- Phan cap do: de/trung/b Kho

**Bien the:**
- **Magic Ball:** thay hop bang mot qua bong pha le (decorative) — giao dien "tuyet mat" hon
- **Trivia Grab Bag:** hoc sinh chon so thay vi cau hoi cu the, giao dien bat ngo hon

---

### 8. Bang Hoa (Gallery Walk Quiz)

**Cach hoat dong:** Cac cau hoi/ket qua duoc dan len tuong phong hoc. Hoc sinh di ra ngoai, di chuyen qua cac tram, viet dap an. Diem tich luy theo so cau dung.

**Trang thiet bi:**
- Analog: giay A3/A4 + but dau (marker)
- Digital: Google Slides nhin chung, Padlet, Nearpod

**Tuy chinh:**
- So tram: 5–15 tram tuy lop
- Thoi gian o moi tram: 1–3 phut
- Co the cho phep lam nhom (2–3 nguoi)

**Bien the:**
- **QR Code Gallery:** cau hoi ma hoa QR, hoc sinh quet diem
- **Musical Gallery Walk:** nhac chay, hoc sinh di chuyen theo nhac, nhac dung tai tram nao thi tra loi tai do
- **Sticky Note Voting:** hoc sinh danh gia cau tra loi cua ban bang cach dan sticky note (xanh/dequivalent)

## III. Tro Choi Thi Dau Dong Doi

### 9. Jeopardy (Bac Si Tri Tue)

**Cach hoat dong:** Bang dien tu gom cac chu de + muc do kho (100–500 diem). Lop chia doi (2 doi). Mot doi chon chu de + muc diem, doi kia doc cau hoi. Tra loi dung duoc diem; tra loi sai mat quyen tra loi.

**Trang thiet bi:**
- Analog: bang giay lon + but dau (ve theo kieu Jeopardy)
- Digital: JeopardyLabs.com, Factile, Canva Jeopardy template, Google Slides template

**Tuy chinh:**
- So doi: 2–4 doi
- So chu de: 3–6 chu de
- Muc do: co the them muc "Daily Double" ngau nhien

**Bien the:**
- **Mini Jeopardy:** 1 vong 5–6 cau thay vi bang day du, cho tiet on tap ngan
- **Team Jeopardy Race:** hoc sinh tra loi ca nhan thay vi doi — ai nhanh nhat duoc quyen chon cau tiep theo

---

### 10. Tro Choi Đoán Từ (Taboo / Headbands)

**Cach hoat dong:**
- **Taboo:** nguoi mo ta tu chinh ma KHONG duoc dung 5 tu "cam" (taboo words) in tren the
- **Headbands:** nguoi doi deo theo sau lung, nhin thay tu cua doi phuong nhung khong biet tu nao — doi phuong mo ta de nguoi do doan

**Trang thiet bi:**
- Analog: the giay in tu + tu taboo; Headbands band (costume)
- Digital: Pear Deck (co che "Draw It"), Gimkit (che do Taboo tu thiet ke)

**Tuy chinh:**
- So nguoi: 4+ (choi doi doi)
- Thoi gian: 60–90 giay moi luot
- Diem: +10 diem/tu dung, -5 neu dung tu taboo

**Bien the:**
- **Math Taboo:** tu chinh la phep tinh, tu taboo la cac so trong phep tinh
- **Vocabulary Headbands:** tu chinh la tu vung, nguoi doan phai giai nghia

---

### 11. Scattergories (Danh Sach Ngau Nhien)

**Cach hoat dong:** Lop chia doi. Mot chu cai duoc chon ngau nhien. Cac doi co 60 giay de viet cac tu bat dau bang chu cai do, thuoc mot danh muc cho truoc (ví du: "con vat", "mon an", "quoc gia").

**Trang thiet bi:**
- Analog: giay + but, chu cai quay so
- Digital: Google Slides shared doc, Pictionary style online tools

**Tuy chinh:**
- So danh muc: 3–6 danh muc
- So vong: 3–5 vong, moi vong chu cai moi
- So nguoi/nhom: 2–6 nhom

**Bien the:**
- **Speed Scattergories:** giam thoi gian xuong 30 giay, tang toc do tu duy
- **Combo Scattergories:** noi cac tu lai thanh cau (ví du: danh muc "hanh dong" → "con cho di bo")

---

### 12. Chuyen Doi Tri (Relay Race / Tri-Race)

**Cach hoat dong:** Cac doi xep hang. Moi thanh vien lan luot chay ra, giai mot bai toan hoac tra loi cau hoi roi chuyen cho nguoi tiep theo. Dong doi nhanh nhat hoan thanh tro choi.

**Trang thiet bi:**
- Analog: bang giay bai tap, but
- Digital: Google Forms timed quiz cho tung nguoi, hoac Kahoot team mode

**Tuy chinh:**
- So nguoi/nhom: 4–6 nhom, moi nhom 4–6 em
- So cau hoi = so thanh vien (moi nguoi 1 cau)

**Bien the:**
- **Math Relay:** bai toan cua nguoi sau phu thuoc dap an cua nguoi truoc (tao ra bai toan lien ket)
- **Vocabulary Relay:** nguoi sau phai viet tu lien quan ve mat nghia cua tu nguoi truoc

---

### 13. Tìm Kho (Scavenger Hunt / Knowledge Hunt)

**Cach hoat dong:** Hoc sinh tim kiem dap an trong phong hoc (dap an duoc giau o cac vi tri khac nhau). Moi dap an la mot phan noi dung hoac goi y cho cau tiep theo.

**Trang thiet bi:**
- Analog: phieu giay giau o cac vi tri, goi y tren bang
- Digital: GooseChase, Actionbound, Padlet (trail mode)

**Tuy chinh:**
- So luong goi y: 5–15 goi y
- Thoi gian: 15–45 phut
- Co the choi ca nhan hoac nhom

**Bien the:**
- **Math Scavenger Hunt:** dap an la ket qua phep tinh, goi y dan den bai toan tiep theo
- **QR Code Hunt:** quet ma QR de nhan cau hoi tiep theo

## IV. Tro Choi So (Digital Game Platforms)

### 14. Kahoot

**Cach hoat dong:** Giao vien tao bo cau hoi (hoac su dung kho Kahoot co san). Hoc sinh tham gia bang ma game tren trinh duyet/dien thoai. Cau hoi + dap an hien thi tren man hinh lop; hoc sinh tra loi tren thiet bi rieng. Xep hang real-time.

**Trang thiet bi:** Thiet bi so (dien thoai, may tinh bam), man hinh/chieu投影

**Tuy chinh:**
- So cau hoi: 5–20 cau
- Thoi gian moi cau: 5–120 giay
- Che do: Individual / Team / Challenge (asynchronous)
- Co the tao bo cau hoi theo chu de, lop hoc, muc do

**Bien the:**
- **Kahoot Team Mode:** chia nhom + choi cung man hinh (giam chi phi thiet bi)
- **Kahoot Challenge:** gui bo cau hoi cho hoc sinh lam o nha
- **Jumble mode:** sap xep cac buoc theo dung thu tu

---

### 15. Quizizz

**Cach hoat dong:** Tuong tu Kahoot nhung hoc sinh di theo toc do rieng (self-paced). Co the hien thi avatar va animat tren man hinh. Co che "Quizizz Homework" cho bai tap nha.

**Trang thiet bi:** Thiet bi so + man hinh lop/chieu投影

**Tuy chinh:**
- So cau hoi: khong gioi han (thuong 10–30)
- Co che memes/avatars de tang tuong tac
- Bao cao chi tiet hon Kahoot (phan tich tung cau)

**Bien the:**
- **Quizizz Live:** che do dong thoi real-time nhu Kahoot
- **Quizizz Select:** giao vien chi dinh cau hoi hien thi cho tung hoc sinh (phan differentiate)

---

### 16. Gimkit

**Cach hoat dong:** Hoc sinh tra loi cau hoi nhung co the mua power-up giua choi (vi du: nhan 2x diem, bo qua cau kho). Tao cau chuyen dau tu kinh te ngay trong tro choi — tap trung vao quan ly ngu luc.

**Trang thiet bi:** Thiet bi so + man hinh lop

**Tuy chinh:**
- Che do co ban: Classic (cai thien theo thoi gian)
- Che do Kits (tu thiet ke bo cau hoi)
- Che do Jess (che do chi co the tra loi dung hoac sai, ko co power-up)

**Bien the:**
- **Gimkit Team Mode:** nhom cung choi
- **Just Float:** hien thi cau hoi tren man hinh lop, hoc sinh tra loi tren giay (che do offline)

---

### 17. Blooket

**Cach hoat dong:** Giong Kahoot nhung voi nhieu che do choi khac nhau (khong chi la classic quiz). Cac che do: Cafe (lam quan ly quay), Gold Quest (tim vang), Tower of Doom (pha dao), etc.

**Trang thiet bi:** Thiet bi so + man hinh lop

**Tuy chinh:**
- Che do choi: 6+ che do khac nhau (moi vong choi deu khac)
- Co the import bo cau hoi tu Kahoot
- Phu hop voi hoc sinh tu lop 3 tro len

**Bien the:**
- **Blooket as Homework:** assign bai tap asen
- **Blooket Live vs Crypto:** che do so sanh diem giua cac lan choi

---

### 18. WordWall

**Cach hoat dong:** Cong cu tao hoat dong tuong tac (khong chi quiz) bao gom: Matching,排序, Open the Box, Penalty Shootout, Random Wheel, ardh card. Phu hop voi on tap tu vung va kien thuc fact-based.

**Trang thiet bi:** Thiet bi so + man hinh lop

**Tuy chinh:**
- Co san 40+ mau hoat dong (templates)
- So nguoi choi: 1–50 nguoi
- Co the thiet ke hoat dong bang tay (in ra) hoac choi online

**Bien the:**
- **WordWall Penalty Shootout:** hoc sinh su dung kien thuc de da bong (moi cau dung = 1 ban thang)
- **WordWall Random Wheel:** quay de chon hoc sinh hoac cau hoi

## V. Tro Choi Khac

### 19. Exit Ticket Games (Tro Choi Kiem Tra Cuoi Tiết)

**Cach hoat dong:** Cuoi tiet hoc, hoc sinh tra loi 1–3 cau hoi nhanh tren phieu ra ve (exit ticket). Nhung thay vi cham thuong, bien thanh tro choi: hoc sinh tra loi + nop, gap so, so sanh voi ban.

**Trang thiet bi:**
- Analog: phieu giay nho
- Digital: Google Forms, Socrative, Flip (Exit Ticket)

**Tuy chinh:**
- So cau hoi: 1–3 cau (de deo)
- Cho diem hoac khong cho diem (chi kiem tra)

**Bien the:**
- **3-2-1 Exit Card:** hoc sinh viet 3 dieu hoc duoc, 2 cau hoi con thac mac, 1 dieu muon chia se
- **Pass the Paper:** hoc sinh tra loi roi chuyen giay, cuoi cung so sanh tat ca cac dap an

---

### 20. Trivia Championship (Thi Tu Van Tri Tue)

**Cach hoat dong:** Lop chia thanh cac dong doi nho (3–5 em). Cac vong thi: Vong 1 (cau hoi ca nhan), Vong 2 (cau hoi nhom), Vong 3 (bau chon). Diem cong don, dong doi co diem cao nhat thang.

**Trang thiet bi:**
- Analog: giay + but
- Digital: Trivia Crack app, QuizUp, Ahaslides

**Tuy chinh:**
- So vong: 3–5 vong
- So cau hoi moi vong: 5–10 cau
- Co the them vong "buzzer" (nhan nut nhanh hon doi phuong)

**Bien the:**
- **Lightning Round:** 10 cau, moi cau 10 giay, diem gap 3
- **Jackpot Round:** mot cau duy nhat, diem gap 5

## VII. De Xuat Tich Hop Vao NavSlides Editor

### Che Do Gamification cho Presentation Editor

Dua tren nghien cuu, cac tinh nang gamification co the tich hop vao NavSlides Editor:

1. **Random Student Picker** — tich hop vong quay ten/truc tiep vao thanh cong cu (toolbar), giao vien co the goi ngau nhien hoc sinh khi trinh chieu
2. **Interactive Quiz Slide** — cho phep tao cac slide co cau hoi tuong tac (dang Kahoot/Quizizz) nhu mot loai slide dac biet trong presentation
3. **Live Polling/Survey** — hien thi cau hoi len man hinh, hoc sinh tra loi qua dien thoai (ket hop voi menti.com hoac tu xay)
4. **Leaderboard Overlay** — lop diem real-time hien thi tren man hinh khi chay buoi on tap
5. **Game Mode Presets** — cac mau san sang (templates) cho Jeopardy, Four Corners, Relay Race, v.v. de giao vien chi can dien noi dung

### Muc tieu Uu tien

| Thu tu | Tinh nang | Do kho | Ghi chu |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Random Student Picker (vong quay) | Thap | Co nhieu thu vien so sanh (Wheel of Names API) |
| 2 | Interactive Quiz Slide type | Trung binh | Can thiet ke interaction model |
| 3 | Live Polling (embedded) | Trung binh | Co the tich hop Menti/Socrative |
| 4 | Leaderboard Overlay | Cao | Can trang thai (state) phuc tap |
| 5 | Game Mode Templates | Cao | Gom nhieu component |

## VIII. Cac Cau Hoi Chua Duoc Giai Quyet

- Khong co nguon web (web search tra ve rong), toan bo noi dung dua tren kien thuc co so cua mo hinh. De xac minh xu huong 2025–2026, can truy cap cac trang Education.com, Common Sense Media, EdSurge, hoac WeAreTeachers.
- Chua nghien cuu chi phi hao (licensing) cua cac platform nhu Kahoot, Gimkit cho moi truong pho thong Vietnam (phien ban mien phi co han che).
- Chua khao sat nhu cau thuc te cua giao vien Vietnam — de xuat khao sat nho trong lan tiep theo.
- Tinh nang "Real-time response" can xem xet ve phap ly & bao mat du lieu hoc sinh (GDPR/PIPL).
