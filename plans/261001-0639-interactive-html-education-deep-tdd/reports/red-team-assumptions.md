## H4 — Trivia migration silently replaces a source scoring product policy
- **Contradictory instruction:** phase-05-existing-games.md:23 acknowledges points-on-buzz, but :104,:115,:123 mandates that buzzing never scores and only answers award points. phase-12-live-server.md:53 and phase-13-live-player.md:51 repeat the replacement. No explicit product decision or migration policy authorizes removing the existing buzz award.
- **Failure scenario:** an existing Trivia lesson migrated to the new runtime changes its winner/leaderboard under the same accepted buzz sequence: it previously awarded +10 on buzz, but now awards zero unless a later answer is accepted. Adding authored rounds and real answer controls does not require erasing that policy.
- **Code evidence:** client/src/components/canvas/element-renderers/game-interactive/trivia-championship-live-game-renderer-with-round-tabs-lightning-jackpot.jsx:113-125 accepts a buzz, locks it, immediately adds 10 to the named player's score and later releases the lock. The source does not condition that award on correctness.
- **Exact proposed patch:** replace the unconditional no-buzz-award requirements in phases05/12/13 with an explicit Trivia scoring configuration, including buzzPoints. Normalize existing Trivia decks to buzzPoints:10; award an accepted buzz once through the same authoritative ledger, with correctness-based points and lightning/jackpot modifiers defined separately. Answer-only mode remains available through an explicit authored zero buzzPoints policy, not a silent migration default. Add source-policy parity acceptance for accepted, duplicate, wrong-answer and replay sequences; update phase03 authoring/schema/defaults to preserve the policy.
